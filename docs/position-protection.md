# Owned position protection and close recovery

The configured execution owner alone installs, verifies, cancels and replaces protection. HTTP requests enqueue reviewed closes; imports and HTTP workers never perform private calls. Protection groups and immutable observations retain original entry configuration, quantity, list and leg identities. A successful entry remains a successful entry when its subsequent protection fails.

Paper groups are labelled `simulation`. The owner applies deterministic full fills, quote fees, adverse slippage and weighted-average basis. Fresh quotes and validated completed execution candles can trigger exits. A whole candle must begin after installation; an entry candle has unknown intrabar ordering. When both thresholds touch one eligible candle, stop wins. Stop gaps use the worse opening price; take-profit fills conservatively use the target. Repeated observations cannot repeat a terminal exit. Partial manual sales replace the simulated pair for the remaining holding using its original entry rules.

Exchange groups use the Spot OCO endpoint with SELL TAKE_PROFIT and STOP_LOSS market-trigger legs, each with the same actual confirmed sellable quantity. Symbol OCO capability, both supported order types, quantity/price filters and a legal trigger range are required. No STOP_MARKET, trailing stop or emergency liquidation is sent. Fees paid in base reduce both confirmed holdings and the protective quantity. A sub-step remainder or crossed trigger creates an explicit failed state rather than an oversized sell or invented liquidation. This design cannot guarantee stop execution price.

Stable list and distinct leg identities are persisted before submission. `active` requires querying the actual list and both leg orders, validating identities, types, triggers and quantities and reconciling actual fills. Placement/cancel acknowledgements alone are insufficient. Lost responses retain identity/reservations and block entries; recovery queries the original list rather than submitting another. Verified provider order IDs retain leg ownership when Binance changes canceled client IDs. Unknown external orders/lists remain operator review incidents; their ownership is never guessed.

Request-budget admission precedes the atomic dispatch marker and active base reservation. Prepared legs without a durable attempted marker are proven not dispatched, including after a crash; their failed local group can be reviewed and reduced without querying an invented provider list. A deferred cancellation leaves the confirmed pair active and the close queued. Only an admitted cancellation records `canceling`; transmitted uncertainty still requires authoritative resolution. Fresh order-review expiry continues to apply after a long budget wait.

Manual sales resolve and cancel the owned pair, query both legs and the list, merge race fills, reconcile confirmed account availability and only then submit a market reduction. A cancellation timeout is unresolved: no conflicting market sell follows it and no automatic repeated cancel grants permission. Partial closes retain and replace the remaining holding's policy. A full close may reduce its dispatch quantity to the reconciled remainder, never above the original reviewed quantity; an explicit partial quantity exceeding the remainder needs a fresh review. Different close request IDs cannot double-sell a flat holding.

An unresolved buy's confirmed partial holding can be protected while its unfilled quote reservation remains retained. New entries stay blocked until the buy outcome is reconciled. A protection failure, unknown list or coverage mismatch blocks new entries. The authenticated dashboard exposes states, quantities, leg states and the next action. `protection_alert` records are durable operator incident evidence; external webhook delivery/configuration belongs to BT14 and is not activated here.

For an uncertain list/cancel, pause entries and inspect/reconcile the exact recorded list and leg IDs; wait for authoritative terminal observations and fills before a conflicting reduction. A confirmed pre-engine protection rejection or local validation failure permits a reviewed manual reduction without claiming that protection exists. There is no automatic liquidation or retry of an ambiguous protection submission. Retain financial history and reservations during recovery. Graceful stop and entry pause never cancel native protection. Restart takes kernel ownership and verifies provider protection before further dispatch.

Offline fixtures and replaced raw CCXT transports establish local behavior only. BT15 must separately prove actual Spot sandbox placement, leg queries, trigger/fill/counterpart cancellation, partial-close reprotection and recovery. Deployment/mainnet activation remain separately authorized gates.
